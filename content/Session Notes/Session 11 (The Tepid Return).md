#TheRock

- We arrive at THE ROCK
- The [[Barden Brouxdouck|Captain]] thanks us for our services and gives us 130 gp each
	- He offers his ships for whenever we may need it for travel
	- He also says he’d pay us again if we choose to accompany him on the return trip 
- Ask captain about places to check out
	- Captain going to the [[Happy Beholder]]—an budget inn
	- [[Man o'War]] - fancy hotel
	- The [[Royal Theater Company ]]- fancy entertainment
	- The [[Arena of Fun]] - low-brow entertainment 
	- Get a ferry to the underside - very pretty 
- We decide to get accommodations at the Happy Beholder
- We walk into the tavern and hear a parrot somewhere in the distance
	- This terrifies [[Twoflower]] because I’ve decided this is my gimmick for this session
	- The parrot (named Nethys) belongs to this creepy tentacle eye guy called “[[Large Luigi]]”
	- Large Luigi is a beholder
		- When Twoflower complains about the parrot, Luigi does a truth scan on Twoflower and verifies that they aren’t lying about their deep-seated fear of parrots, and then sends the parrot away 
		- Large Luigi then decided to make his parrot invisible to solve Twoflower’s anxiety. This only makes it worse. 
			- [[Scribe]] gives Twoflower a bit of her armor to use as a shield against the invisible parrot. 
- Scribe secures a room for the night at the Happy Beholder and also sinks some of her money into Holy Volley, very expensive alcohol
	- Large Luigi makes the parrot reappear in front of Twoflower, giving Twoflower a heart attack and they proceed to hit it with the armor Scribe gave to them
	- They decide to leave the tavern before Twoflower dies of shock
- The party heads to the [[Arena of Fun]] 
- As we walk through the markets, we run into [[Omsoc]] (the crocodile man who we met at the devil party)
	- Twoflower buys the Necklace of Knives (renamed to Necklace of Nethys-Slaying)
	- Scribe buys the Disguise Scarf
	- [[Salem]] buys like speed boots and a water-proof journal (?) I forgot
	- Before we leave, Omsoc sells Twoflower a net-gun, which would be very useful against the parrot.
