#CityOfBrass

---

- We fight the very angry salamanders, and are able to defeat them after a bit of a struggle
- Afterwards, we have no choice but to jump into the caldera of the volcano where apparently the entrance of the portal is
- We end up in a tunnel that looks to be carved out. On one side there’s an intense heat, and on the other we hear the sound of work and machinery
	- We head towards the sound of work to try to figure out where we ended up
- Meet an aezir (like a fire gnome) guy, [[Hyrayr]], who is just really not happy to see us
	- We get to talking with him and mentions he mysteriously got an order 2 months ago (same time period when all the weird stuff happened to [[Vodahmin]] and [[Salem]], among others)
	- The buyer appeared human, but was not at all sweating in the intense heat, so maybe not human. Tall, broadly built.
		- Had some other people that came with them
	- Asked for some kind of clamping apparatus, on a three-legged tripod, made with a metal he was not at all familiar with
	- Doesn’t remember the name, something like “Krii Se Ov” or “Khrusaor”
		- “Khrusaor” sounds like an angelic name that means “Golden Sword”
		- “Krii Se Ov” sounds like a draconic name that means “Killing of Trust”
- We head on out of Hyrayr’s workshop and signal a lava ship on over to hitch a ride
	- The ship that picked us up is captained by an air genasi named Samir
	- He kindly turns his ship around and takes us to the City of Brass, which is a 15 minute trip away 
- We head for an inn called the Burning Pit, named for its prominent burning pit
	- We ask the innkeeper about the [[Wardens of Ragathiel]]
		- Seems to be well liked by the people, but not so much by the ruling Sultans. Vigilante types.
		- Seen them around the Fire Blossom Palace
	- [[Twoflower]] & Salem share a room together. 
		- Agree to look into leads on Twoflower’s clone flower [[Pentamerous|Penty]]
		- Salem confirms he was **not a prostitute**. It was more accurately described as a sugar daddy situation. 
	- Also, Twoflower takes the evil orb from Scribe, to confirm whether or not Scribe’s vision were connected to her possession of the Evil Orb
