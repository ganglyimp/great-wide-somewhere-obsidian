#Sigil

---
- [[Twoflower]] wakes up early and heads downstairs. [[Barkus]] sees them and waves them over
	- Says a gnoll has been loitering around the bar, hanging around the cellar door 
	- Hasn’t talked or interacted with anyone. Currently sitting in a booth, blankly staring at nothing
- Twoflower heads back upstairs to grab everyone else before confronting the gnoll. By the time they come back out, the gnoll has left the bar
- We head outside to check the perimeter of the building, just to double check
	- We see the gnoll at the cellar door, chipping away at the wood of the door with a knife
	- When we try to call out, the gnoll hisses at us, her eyes glassy
	- After she carved out a small hole in the corner of the door, she abruptly turns around and walks away
- We try tailing her, but she sees us and starts bolting. After a short chase, we end up in a dead end at the end of an alleyway
	- And then we are attacked by swarms of cranium rats. Gross little fuckers with their brains hanging out
	- We manage to kill them all and remove their hold on the gnoll
		- [[Vodahmin]] burns through a metric ton of rats and we dub him “Rat Burner, Scourge of Rats”
	- The gnoll’s name is [[Violence]]
		- Doesn’t remember much, besides the rats wanted world conquest, starting with control of the pantries all across Sigil
		- The rats wanted their base of operations near their sworn enemies, [[Parakk Pest]], the exterminators
		- She’s probably involved in the fighting pits
- We head towards Parakk Pest to inform them about the cranium rat infestation
	- We meet up with a giff, and share the information with him 
	- Says that it seems like we killed most of them, but might still be more to investigate
	- Vodahmin offered a full time job as an exterminator, he politely declines, but leaves our contact info if they have any updates on the cranium rats
- We spend some time clearing Violence’s name (since during the chase, we were yelling out “THIEF” at her)
	- Meet up again with Violence. Find out she’s a bounty hunter and an interplanar guide. We have her as a contact if we ever need help with anything like that in the future.
