#SwordCoast 

---

- After a brief fight, we take down [[Reverence]]’s guards and he then quickly surrenders, having done nothing but struggle in [[Vodahmin]]’s grapple
	- Said he was trying to open a gateway to the hells for his new patron, who calls himself “The Lord of Deals”
	- [[Salem]] recalls that that moniker is used by a large number of devils and is fairly generic
- And now there’s a big tear into the planes of hell inside of this cave
	- We talk to [[Reidoth]] and he says he’ll call over some other druids to help close up the portal
	- Reverence says that he found a crystal orb when robbing a dead guy’s house. It’s an arcane focus. After picking it up, he started to hear the voice and get visions from the “Lord of Deals,” and was offered great powers and immortality in exchange for doing his bidding
	- We decide to hold Reverence prisoner as we wait for the other druids to arrive
	- We collapse the tunnel to prevent anything from coming through
	- Once the other druids arrive, we decide to leave Reverence (now tied up suspended in a tree) to them and they work on closing the portal.
	- We keep the orb, held by [[Scribe]], for now. 
- Travel to Mount Hotenow towards a portal to the City of Brass (in the Plane of Fire)
- On our way there, we stop by Neverwinter to do a bit of shopping
	- When we rest at Neverwinter, Scribe is assaulted by a vision of the Scribes assaulting a city of gold. An imposing figure glances at her before she wakes up
		- She goes to Salem to help her analyze this dream
		- They decide it’s a message from Ragathiel, but the message still feels unclear whether it’s direction or a warning
- We begin to ascend the volcano, and get a sense of fire elemental energy pouring out from the heart of the volcano
	- We notice some giant glowing red snakes swimming in the magma
- Scribe and Vodahmin approach the two serpents, to make an attempt to say hi before we start blindly attacking them
	- They run into [[Omsoc]], who is just running a shop inside the heart of a volcano
	- He confirms that there’s a portal to the Plane of Fire at the heart of the volcano
	- He offers us some potions of fire resistance to help us in the plane
- After concluding business with Omsoc, he quickly departs, leaving us to deal with the two fire serpents
