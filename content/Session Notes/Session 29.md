**Date**: 10/05/25
#SwordCoast #SideQuestPalooza

---
- On our way to Agatha’s Lair, we encounter a small group of armed orcs who tell us to “drop our weapons”
	- Says they need to make way for “The Great Many Arrows” ([[Helga]] says they’re an orc tribe to the East)
	- Also says they’re definitely trying to rob us
	- [[Vodahmin]] scares them away with a plume of fire. As they’re running away they yell in orcish, “Dragon! Another dragon! Run!”
- We were tasked with clearing out some orcs on the road, so we decide to pursue them
	- We follow them to a camp they set up
	- We all put on a grand aura-farming performance of intimidation, [[Scribe]] putting on Rapture-voice, backlit by flames from Vodahmin, with [[Salem]]’s war drums echoing in the background. And the orcs all collectively shit themselves
	- Vodahmin questions the orcs about the thing about “another dragon”
		- Says they were driven out from their home by a dragon, and they have since been searching for a new home
		- Says the dragon had white scales, “eyes that froze my friend solid”, “very big”
	- Vodahmin asks, “Have you ever considered a different career path?”
		- They say, “Yeah, sure, buddy, whatever you want.”
	- The orcs say they have also been investigating the undead at Old Owl Well
- With the orcs now in our party, we decide to join them in scouting out Old Owl Well
	- See a striped tent near the entrance of a crumbling tower
	- Orcs report they saw a “walking corpse” tied by the neck to the axle of the well
	- [[Twoflower]] scouts the tower and sees an assortment of undead, and the tent appears to be occupied by the necromancer. We decide not to investigate further for now
- We leave Old Owl Well and head towards the orcs’ base of operations (Wyven Tor)
	- We are not allowed in unless our hands our tied up and we’re led in as prisoners (we decide to agree with this)
	- When we meet with the leader of the orcs, he’s not at all intimidated by our theatrics and immediately engages in combat
	- And then he immediately dies in combat
- The scoutmaster, Gaurim, says he hears the message loud and clear, and says they will not settle in this area
