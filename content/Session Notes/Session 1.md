**Date**: 03/30/25
#Sigil

---

- [[Barkus]] (from [[The Smoldering Corpse]]) gives us a job board
	- We decide to investigate a job from a person named [[Pearl]]. Pretty vague but offering 50gp.
- Pearl has been dealing with moss overgrowth in her basement. Tried burning it away, but it keeps on coming back. She thinks there’s a creature down there that is causing the moss issue. 
	- Pearl is also a retired adventurer, used to be an alchemist 
- As we traverse the basement, we follow the trail of mold, and find a note from a person named Artie saying “Hey Pearl, clean up this room please”
- Lots of runic traps and ritualistic magic circles
	- Find a note from [[Artie]] saying that he’s been doing a lot of dangerous rituals
	- Some scroll papers come to life and fold themselves into sentient origami
		- We try talking to it, and they imply that Artie is temporarily out somewhere, but will be returning soon
- We run into the slime mold hiding underneath a table
	- We are able to defeat it 
- We come across a room with a magic mirror. On the other side is a lush forest and a weird ghost dog
	- We befriend the ghost dog, it ascends to the ethereal realm, and leaves behind some ghost charges
- Returning to Pearl, we receive our money. Apparently she didn’t know Artie (her old friend) was crashing in her basement.
