**Date**: 04/21/24
#ElysiumBall #Sigil

---

- Coming out of [[Pearl]]’s house, we make our way to the Elysium Ball, hosted in [[The Lady’s Ward]] by [[Erudyne Vaughn]]
	- The Lady’s Ward is super gentrified and elegant, very quiet and orderly 
- As we enter the manor, we are approached by a beautiful woman in a lovely dress
	- She asks us if we would like to accept a glamor to make us look more acceptable
	- Seems a bit disappointed when she hears that we found a flyer for the ball at [[The Smoldering Corpse]] tavern 
	- She asks us, very discreetly, to meet us in the upstairs bathroom in an hour
	- Her name is [[Bostin]] 
- Erudyne says that we’re welcome to explore only the banquet hall and the back gardens. All other areas a off limits
- [[Salem]] runs into [[Omsoc]], a large crocodilian creature
	- An Illiderian, a being of pure chaos. Tend to be obsessed with magic objects or objects of curiosity 
	- Salem says he used to be a cleric, but some events happened that changed that recently 
	- Omsoc is very excited that Salem has been having some memory problems (because he’ll be able to experience everything again)
	- Recently lost his connection to his god(?)
- [[Scribe]] runs into a woman adorned with rich jewels, an air elemental named Khadija
	- Khadija says she doesn’t get up to much these days, her family have a seat in the Plane of Air, so a lot of generational wealth 
	- She said she made all the jewelry that she’s wearing; sometimes sells her jewelry for a bit of pocket money
	- Scribe encourages her to pursue her jewelry making dreams
- [[Twoflower]] tries their hand at some of the carnival game
	- Manages to lose like 5sp to the games and storms off in embarrassment 
- [[Vodahmin]] runs into an orc named Taul
	- He’s from Faerun, used to live in a hill with his many brothers
	- Got lost in the storm, found his way to a big cavern in the mountains, and eventually passed through a shimmery crack in the wall, and ended up in Planescape
	- Vodahmin gives Taul his life story 
- Salem meets [[Twoflower]] at the fair games
	- He tries the archery game, despite Twoflower’s incessant warnings against it
	- Somehow, Salem manages to win a cantrip deck of cards
- Salem meets up with Scribe, and they both decide to go off to try their hand at the archery game again 
	- Scribe hits the strength game so hard, she breaks the machine, easily winning the 5gp
	- At the archery game, Salem sees that the arrows are weighted
		- Scribe intimidates the robot into replacing the arrows with normal arrows
		- She wins a “lesser dwarven daisy”, which is a string of firecrackers
- Twoflower breaks off from them, and joins Vodahmin to start drinking alcohol (for the first time) copiously 
- Finally, the moment strikes for us all to meet Bostin in the bathroom. We let Scribe sneak up first
