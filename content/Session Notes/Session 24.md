**Date**: 07/20/25
#SwordCoast

---
- We find [[Sildar Halwinter|Sildar]] in the bar, and begin talking to him about what happened
	- Sildar asks if we’d be interested in trying to track down the missing Dwarven brothers who seem to be of interest to the [[Redbrands]]
	- These dwarves are trying to find an ancient fabled mines of great fortune
	- The dwarves tried to set up a small base outside of Phandalin. Apparently they had a map to the ancient mines
- We decide to head back to the bugbears in the manor basement, with [[Scribe|Mitra]] disguised as [[Glasstaff]]
	- We also decide to decapitate a dead Redbrand to pass it off as [[Able]] when going back to talk to [[Clarg]]
	- Mitra manages to trick the Bugbears to leave and go back to the castle
	- She convinces them to leave behind a goblin ([[Droop]]) instead of eating him
		- She talks to Droop and convinces him to give up his thieving ways a pursue a life of kindness
- After leaving the [[Tresendar Manor|manor]] , we check the surrounding woods to see if we can intercept the scouting goblins
	- We track them down.
	- They say they directly report to [[King Groll]], who works for [[The Black Spider]] at [[Cragmore Castle]]
	- The terrified goblins spill the full directions to Cragmore Castle
	- We also ask about what people they’ve seen coming and going. They confirm that they’ve seen Abel enter town, but not leave. 
- As we return to town, we notice Droop hiding up in a tree
	- Apparently he tried to pick some apples from an orchard and give it to the orchard’s farmer. Who then tried to murder Droop
	- We go to Sildar and ask him to take Droop under his wing to prevent things like this from occurring again
	- Mitra also tells [[Helga]] and Abel to lay low in town to prevent any of Clarg’s goblins from noticing our lie (about passing off a Redbrand head as Abel’s head)
- We then decide to head out to Clarg’s cave to deliver the head
	- We have to camp in the woods for the night. During Scribe’s watch, we get attacked by some Redbrands
	- [[Vodahmin]] eviscerates one of them with a column of flame, and the other Redbrand quickly retreats
