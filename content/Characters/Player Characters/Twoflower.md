- Twoflower is from the pretty side of Bytopia, Dothion
- A few years ago, got a fungal infection in their brain. When they had it checked out by a healer, they were giving the prognosis of half a century. With time running out, they decide they want to see the Planescape before they die.
	- They paid [[Scribe]] all of their life savings to essentially act as their bodyguard and tourguide
- Has an irrational fear of parrots. Especially [[Large Luigi]]'s pet parrot, Nethys. (Source: [[Session 11 (The Tepid Return)]])
- When [[Salem]] and [[Vivian Valentine]] were trying to audition at the [[Royal Theater Company]], Twoflower introduced themselves as their manager to the troupe leader (Source: [[Session 12]])

## Fungal Facts
- When researching the fungal infection at the Library of the Spheres on The Rock (Source: [[Session 13]]):
	- Most fungus native to Bytopia are relatively benign. 
	- Some fungus that is found in Shurrock (the craggy mountain part of Bytopia) may have hailed from other positive or negative planes
	- The vast majority of invasive fungus comes from The Abyss (the chaotic evil demon realm)
	- [[Salem]] is able to scry that there *is* a demon prince of fungus and rot, [[Zuggtmoy]]. It's likely Twoflower's infection is some kind of abyssal corruption
- While [[Vodahmin]] was under the influence of a Spectator's curse (Wave-Echo Cave), noticed a large red, spiritual splotch on the back of Twoflower’s neck, with tendrils spreading throughout their body (Source: [[Session 35]])
	- By Salem's estimation, Twoflower's life expectancy has lowered to around 40-45 years
- [[Irene]] suggests finding a healer on a positive plane of lawful alignment, like Arcadia. (Source: [[Session 39]])
	- She believes that since the infection is near Twoflower's brain, it's possible that it could result in changes in their personality as it spreads, making them more violent and irrational
	- Seems to be the work of [[Zuggtmoy]], a demonic prince. Best to avoid the lower planes where her influence is stronger.
- [[Sir Elric]] offers Twoflower some avenues to help deal with the demonic function (Source: [[Session 41]])
	- Can awaken it by giving it offerings of mortal blood, which can be done by just drinking some of it
	- Advises against surgical removal, as that can be risky and result in lower quality of life. Exorcism is equally risky.
	- He believes the best path forward is to bind it in a ritual and turn it into a servant of Twoflower.
