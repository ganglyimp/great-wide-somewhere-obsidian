## Ragathiel
- Information found at The Library of the Spheres on The Rock (Source: [[Session 13]]): 
	- Ragathiel is a being of duty, vengeance, and knighthood. Also associated with camaraderie and brotherhood. 
	- Lifts people up and brings light to his allies. Takes in not just knights and crusaders, but also takes in the marginalized and the falsely accused. 
	- Seems to be way less zealous than the version Scribe was brought up with 
- [[Irene]] (of the [[Wardens of Ragathiel]]) notes that it's strange that the leader of the Scribes referred to herself as "the General" as that's usually a title reserved for Ragathiel himself (Source: [[Session 38]])
